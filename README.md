# August Edward Winter

**The travels of August Edward Winter, Lutheran Missionary (1838–1927)**

A narrative archive of Winter’s life and missionary journey—especially the years 1861–1865 on the Minnesota frontier—based on his 1899 published account and family records.

## Major upgrade (Astro)

This branch rebuilds the site in **Astro** for a faster, cleaner static archive:

- Archival paper aesthetic and refined typography
- Portrait + four-generation family photo that load reliably from `/public`
- Full narrative timeline with quotes, key facts, and location chips
- Sources page and journey-at-a-glance path cards
- Mobile-friendly CSS-only navigation
